***Root Cafe***

_Looking to chill out with a soothing cup? Take a seat in the Root Cafe, and enjoy a hot beverage or two!

 As the cafe's master barista, you'll pour a ton of restorative drinks, from bold hot brews to decadent hot chocolates, each specially prepared for your adorable residents.

Even though we're beginners, we made this cosy little game in only three days._

Hope you enjoy it! :D

**Key Features:**

- Cute, hand-drawn animals!

- Fun little recipe book

- Drink making system (With physics!)

- An original soundtrack

**How to Play:**
-See a customers order.
-Press "Enter Kitchen" and press the recipe book button to find what they ordered
-Prepare the customer's drink with the minimum requirements of each ingredient from the recipe. To do this, click and drag the ingredients (you can also fling them around your screen) and pour them into the cup.
-When the recipe requires coffee, just put the cup under the coffee machine and it fills up automatically.
-When you think your drink is done, press "serve drink" at the top of the screen.
Repeat this until all customers are served!
